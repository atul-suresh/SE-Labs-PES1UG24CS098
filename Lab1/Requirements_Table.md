# Requirements Table

## Smart Parking Space Rental & License Validator

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall allow a Vehicle Owner to view currently available parking bays and reserve an available bay for a selected parking period. | High | **Pass:** The selected available bay is successfully reserved and is no longer shown as available for the reserved period. **Fail:** The same bay can be simultaneously reserved by multiple users. | Real-time reservation prevents parking conflicts and improves parking-space utilization. |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall capture the vehicle license plate at the parking entrance and validate it against an active reservation before permitting entry. | High | **Pass:** A vehicle with a valid matching reservation is successfully verified. **Fail:** A vehicle without a valid matching reservation is approved. | License-plate validation ensures that only authorized vehicles use reserved parking spaces. |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall record the vehicle entry time when a valid vehicle enters the parking facility and record the exit time when the vehicle leaves. | High | **Pass:** Correct entry and exit timestamps are stored for the parking session. **Fail:** Entry or exit timestamps are missing or incorrectly recorded. | Accurate timestamps are required for session tracking, billing, and overstay detection. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall calculate the total parking fee upon vehicle exit based on the elapsed parking duration and the applicable progressive hourly pricing tier. | High | **Pass:** The calculated amount correctly corresponds to the recorded parking duration and applicable pricing tier. **Fail:** An incorrect duration or pricing tier is used. | Accurate progressive billing ensures that Vehicle Owners are charged correctly. |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall monitor active parking sessions and flag vehicles that remain parked beyond their authorized reservation period for review by the Parking Warden. | High | **Pass:** A vehicle exceeding its authorized parking period is flagged for the Parking Warden. **Fail:** An unauthorized overstay is not detected or flagged. | Automatic overstay detection allows Parking Wardens to handle unauthorized parking efficiently. |
| NFR-001 | Performance & Security | The gate-camera license-plate lookup and reservation-validation operation shall complete within 300 milliseconds under normal and simulated peak-load conditions. | High | **Pass:** Validation completes within 300 ms while maintaining required security checks. **Fail:** Validation exceeds 300 ms or bypasses security checks. | Fast and secure validation prevents delays and unauthorized entry at the parking gate. |
| NFR-002 | Reliability | The system shall preserve confirmed parking reservations and recover normal reservation-validation functionality within 60 seconds after a temporary service interruption. | Medium | **Pass:** Confirmed reservations remain intact and validation functionality is restored within 60 seconds. **Fail:** Reservations are lost or recovery exceeds 60 seconds. | Reliable recovery minimizes parking-service disruption and prevents loss of reservation information. |
